Your Canvas assignments, pulled from your calendar feed.

Connect Canvas

In Canvas go to Calendar → Calendar Feed and copy the link. It looks like https://yourschool.instructure.com/feeds/calendars/user_XXXX.ics. The long code in the URL is what lets this page read your assignments. It is saved only in this browser.

Feed won't load? Upload or paste the .ics file instead

Open the feed URL in a new tab, save the file, then choose it here.
